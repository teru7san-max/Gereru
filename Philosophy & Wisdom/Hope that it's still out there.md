# Hope that it's still out there

> [!quote] Passage (exact)
> I truly admire anyone who still has the hope that what they are looking for is still OUT THERE! This means you have HOPE!
> — source not yet recorded

This closes the same passage as [[We want the feeling, not the thing]].

## Meaning

The author admires people who keep believing that what they're looking for (love, meaning, a place that feels like home) still exists and can be found. Keeping that belief alive, especially after disappointment, is what the author calls hope.

## Core insight

Hope keeps me open to what's possible, but it can turn into a search that never lets me arrive.

Монголоор: *Итгэл найдвар биднийг нээлттэй байлгадаг, гэхдээ "жаргал үргэлж өөр газар байгаа" гэсэн эцэсгүй эрэл болж хувирч болно.*

## Critical look

- **A tension inside the passage.** Two lines earlier it says "all the answers lie deep within your soul". Here it praises hope that the answer is "OUT THERE". These can be reconciled: what you look for out there (a person, a path) can be real even if the feeling you hope for from it is partly inside. Still, the passage doesn't resolve the tension itself.
- **Two kinds of hope.**
  - *Open* hope keeps me trying, staying curious and not giving up on something meaningful.
  - *Deferring* hope believes fulfilment is always somewhere else, later, and so never lets me experience what is already here.

  The same words can describe either one.

## Connections

- `hope` ↔ `presence`: the open question is how to keep hope alive without living in the future.
- `hope` ↔ `desire`: deferring hope is close to the trap described in [[We want the feeling, not the thing]].

## Back to life

> Is my hope right now keeping me open, or keeping me from noticing what's already here?
