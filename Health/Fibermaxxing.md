# Fibermaxxing

> [!summary] TL;DR
> **Fibermaxxing** = *эслэгийн хэрэглээг зориудаар нэмэгдүүлэх*, which means deliberately increasing how much fiber you eat.
> Add it slowly (+3–5 g per week), drink plenty of water, and aim for **25 g/day (women)** or **38 g/day (men)**.

> [!warning] Don't jump straight to the target
> If you add a lot of fiber at once, you can get bloating, gas and cramps. Ramp up over several weeks and drink more water as you go.

![[fiber-needs-water.svg]]

## The dish shown: bean & cabbage stir-fry 🥬🫘

A quick pan-fried slaw made with:

- [ ] Red kidney beans (cooked and drained)
- [ ] Green cabbage, finely shredded
- [ ] Carrot, julienned
- [ ] Cucumber / zucchini, cut into strips
- [ ] Oil, salt and seasoning to taste

**Method:** rinse the cooked beans in a colander → stir-fry the cabbage, carrot and cucumber in a hot pan until just wilted → fold in the beans → serve.

## My takeaways

- Start from what I eat now and add **one fiber-rich side per day**
- Track the weekly increase: week 1 +3 g → week 2 +6 g → …
- Water bottle always nearby 💧
