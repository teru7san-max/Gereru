# Still Out There

> I truly admire anyone who still has the hope that what they are looking for is still **OUT THERE!**
> This means you have **HOPE!**

## Visual version

![[hope-out-there-visual.gif]]

A short story told almost without words, in four beats:

1. **Searching.** On a dark hilltop, a lone figure raises a lantern and sweeps its beam across the night sky.
2. **It's out there.** The beam settles, and a single gold star lights up far away.
3. **Reaching.** Sparks rise from the lantern and settle into a bridge of stars that arcs up to the gold star.
4. **Hope.** The sun rises, the other stars fade, and the gold star stays bright. A single word appears: *hope*.

## Text version

![[hope-out-there.gif]]

The full quote appears line by line over a starry night. It ends on a glowing **HOPE!** as the lantern flares and dawn begins.
